FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Is Huruf free?

Yes. All 500 levels are free to play. Points for hints can be earned in-game by finishing levels, spinning the daily wheel or watching a video, and buying them is entirely optional.

Does Huruf improve your brain or memory?

Huruf is built as a mental workout: forming words from a scrambled wheel exercises recall, pattern recognition, letter-combination reasoning and sustained focus. Every session also expands your Arabic vocabulary, since each word carries its dictionary meaning.

Do I need an account?

No. The campaign works straight away with no sign-up. The daily challenge, leaderboard and duels create an anonymous profile for you automatically — there is no form to fill in.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The full 500-level campaign and all 62 language challenges work offline. Only the daily challenge, friends leaderboard and duels need a connection.

How does the daily challenge work?

Every player in the world gets the same puzzle each day and can play it once. Finish it on consecutive days and your streak grows; miss a day and it resets to one. The puzzle changes at midnight UTC.

Is Huruf good for learning Arabic?

It builds vocabulary and reading fluency and teaches authentic Arabic proverbs, Gulf sayings and classical poetry. The interface is entirely in Arabic, so it suits people who can already read Arabic script rather than absolute beginners.

How do I add a friend?

Every player gets a six-character friend code. Send yours as plain text on any messaging app, or type in theirs. No links, no permissions, no contact-list access.

Which languages does it support?

The game is entirely in Arabic, written in Gulf dialect and laid out right-to-left throughout. The puzzle vocabulary itself is Modern Standard Arabic.
