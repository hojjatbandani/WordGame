FAQ

Everything you want to know

Is Huruf free?

Yes. Huruf is completely free and all 500 stages can be played without paying. You earn coins by playing, from the daily reward and the lucky wheel, and you can watch an optional video for a bonus. The game contains family-friendly ads.

Is Huruf available in English?

The menus can be switched to English, while the puzzles themselves are always in Arabic — Huruf is an Arabic word game.

Does Huruf work offline?

Yes. Every stage and language challenge works without a connection, so you can play on a plane or anywhere else.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You start playing right away with no sign-up and no forms, and you can optionally connect Google Play Games to save your achievements.

Is Huruf suitable for children?

Huruf is designed for the whole family: meaningful language content with no violence, and ads limited to general-audience content that can be closed within seconds.

Does Huruf help with learning Arabic?

It grows your vocabulary, because every correct word appears with its meaning, and it introduces Arabic and Gulf proverbs and classic poetry. It suits people who can already read Arabic script best.

How many stages does Huruf have?

500 stages across 8 worlds, with letter wheels of 3 to 8 letters, plus 62 special language challenges.

Which devices does Huruf run on?

Huruf is available now on Android devices through Google Play.
